Measurement method
Images are decoded as RGB floats in [0,255]. Edge MAE is mean absolute channel difference between first/last columns or rows. Natural adjacent-pixel MAE is the corresponding mean across every internal neighboring pair. A mortar pixel has R-B < 40 and Rec.709 luminance > 80; a horizontal mortar band is a contiguous set of rows with more than 55% such pixels. Bands and course spacings are therefore approximate segmentation, corroborated by visual inspection. These checks do not prove perfectly matching fine-grain periodic detail.
