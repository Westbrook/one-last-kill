TOUCH CONTROLS

Enable on-screen touch controls in Settings. Drag the left stick to move and swipe the right side to look. Tap FIRE to attack; hold for automatic fire. Drag FIRE to keep looking while firing. Tap AIM, RUN, or CROUCH to toggle them. JUMP, USE, RELOAD, MELEE, DROP, RAGE, and PAUSE handle the other actions. USE picks up weapons and ammunition. Look sensitivity also adjusts touch look.

Melee keeps your firearm and ammunition. Rage unlocks below 30% health after 4 kills in the last 60 seconds. Press T, D-pad up, or the touch RAGE button to double current health. Kill within 10 seconds to keep the remaining boost; otherwise health returns to its starting value. Pausing freezes both clocks.
