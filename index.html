TOUCH CONTROLS

Enable on-screen touch controls in Settings. Touch the left movement area to center the stick, then drag to move. Swipe the right side to look. Tap and release FIRE to attack once, or drag FIRE to aim without firing. RUN and CROUCH toggle on each tap. USE picks up weapons and ammunition.

Tap MOTION during play and allow access to aim by turning or tilting your phone. Once allowed, the controls become compact and all touch-based looking turns off. FIRE becomes a dedicated trigger: press to attack or hold for automatic fire. Hold RECENTER while adjusting your grip, then release. Tap MOTION again to restore the original controls. They stay available without permission and return if motion cannot start. Look sensitivity adjusts both modes.

SIGHTS appears with firearms and toggles a closer view with tighter shot spread. Motion or swiping turns the camera. RAGE appears only when you can activate it. JUMP, RELOAD, MELEE, DROP, and PAUSE handle the other actions.

Melee keeps your firearm and ammunition. Rage unlocks below 30% health after 4 kills in the last 60 seconds. Press T, D-pad up, or the touch RAGE button to double current health. Kill within 10 seconds to keep the remaining boost; otherwise health returns to its starting value. Pausing freezes both clocks.
