# Little Sicily storefront kit — August 31, 2026

`src/render/storefront-kit.js` is original code-authored game art. It uses the existing fictional shop names from `DISTRICT`; no photograph, film still, real business logo, external illustration, or downloaded font is used. Text is painted once into a shared local 1024×1024 canvas atlas with browser-provided Georgia/Arial fallbacks. The atlas contains seven painted shop boards and four small window/closed-shop cards, with inset UVs and eight-pixel cell margins. Small deterministic paint losses are color variation, not baked lighting. There is no image-generation model or prompt to attribute.

The geometry is authored in metres from the existing shared box and pipe primitives. Tall sash, paired casement and broad industrial windows share construction helpers. Sage timber, cream trim, oxblood timber and painted iron give the deli, market, pharmacy, laundry, barber and repair/hardware shops different entrances, proportions and occupancy cues. Blinds and shallow display silhouettes suggest closed premises. These background shops retain their solid building masses and do not become accessible rooms; the bakery retains its established open entrance and warm objective lighting.

Material clones reuse the game's existing color, normal and roughness maps. Brick and plaster ultimately use the local Poly Haven sets by Rob Tuytel under CC0, with the same complete generated/procedural fallback chain recorded in [art direction](art-direction.md#assets-and-source-provenance) and the existing material manifests. Timber, concrete, iron, tar and glass maps are the existing original code-authored materials. The kit does not recolor shared source materials or allocate another copy of their maps.

The sign atlas replaces the thirteen individual 1024×256 shop-name/CLOSED textures. Its uncompressed RGBA8 estimate is 4 MiB at the base level and 5.33 MiB with the full mip chain, compared with 13 MiB / 17.33 MiB for the former sign maps. This is source payload accounting, not measured driver residency. No image file is downloaded for the new atlas. Painting, geometry transformation and per-material merging occur at world startup; no new texture update, geometry construction, light, shadow map or frame-loop callback is introduced during play.

The coherent art review records final browser evidence, accepted revisions and measured runtime costs separately. This source record alone does not establish visual quality or performance.

Construction review rejected the first fascia backing depth: its rear ended 5 mm short of the masonry and the print stood 17 mm ahead of the board. The final 20 cm backing embeds 10 mm into masonry and leaves a 2 mm print separation. This was found by checking the actual transformed surfaces and is covered by the focused contact test; it is not a claim that a screenshot alone established that fit. The adjustment changes no triangles, material groups or textures.
