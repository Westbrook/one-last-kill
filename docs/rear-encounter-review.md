# Rear encounters and attack feedback — 2026-08-28

This pass adds rear pressure to the balcony and stairs, separates balcony arrival positions, and makes unseen attacks readable without sound. It preserves the existing world geometry, checkpoints, floor supplies, total contact budgets and live caps.

Each balcony pair and stair pair reserves its second entry for a possible rear arrival. Balcony anchors use traversed terrace/gallery ground; stair anchors use the bottom landing of the flight the player is climbing. A rear arrival starts at least five metres away, within the authored local distance limit, with floor support and clearance for the complete spawn body. It must be outside the rendered view or fully concealed by one opaque structural box. Nine rays check the body corners and centre against that same solid; separate small blockers cannot hide an exposed gap between the samples. Hidden parents, masked or transparent materials and collision-only screens do not count as cover. If placement fails, the entry waits. A delayed forward fallback remains subject to concealment, stage, distance, collision and crowd checks.

Weapon choice follows the actual arrival direction, even when the player turns away from an intended forward spawn. Fists, a bat, a knife or a firearm with no ammunition allow only an unarmed rear brawler. A firearm with loaded or reserve ammunition allows at most a bat carrier; an authored unarmed contact stays unarmed. The player never receives a weapon from this adjustment. Authored wave compositions are therefore templates rather than guaranteed drop rosters. A new arrival gets a copied investigation target and at least one second before it can begin an attack.

The balcony gallery alternates spawn lanes by 0.56 metres. A separate bearing check prevents same-wave actors from occupying the same narrow aim line: the required separation is 0.4 metres across the nearer viewing ray and 0.04 radians. Opposite sides of the player are already distinct. Candidates that fail the perspective check are deferred. The balcony still has six contacts in three pairs, and the stairwell still has eight contacts in four pairs, with at most two alive in either space.

Stair enemies now follow the authored flights and end landings toward observed positions. Route projection includes elevation, and crossing between lanes happens beyond the guard ends. A melee enemy does not stop below a target and swing through the floor. Rear priming provides one initial observation, not continuous tracking through walls; an unseen investigation still expires within twelve seconds. Passing a landing can retire its pending contacts, but living pursuers keep their identity and occupy their normal live slots.

The new HUD warning shows **ATTACK FROM BEHIND/LEFT/RIGHT/ABOVE/BELOW** in amber during an unseen windup or burst and **HIT FROM …** in red after damage. A rotating arrow gives the bearing and a count identifies multiple sources. The actual rendered camera supplies eye position, yaw, pitch, aim FOV, aspect and zoom. A partly visible body does not trigger an offscreen warning. Recent hits follow live actor identities for at most 1.1 seconds of simulation time; turning to see a source acknowledges its hit. Pause freezes time, and removed/dead actors, player death, completed play and checkpoint restoration clear stale feedback. The HUD adds no sound, flashing animation, scene lights or 3D meshes.

The implementation separates pure placement and warning rules from mission and HUD adapters. Pending entries retain their original slot identity through retries, so a blocked forward actor cannot starve a safe rear actor. Adaptive type/count changes commit only after the rig pool succeeds. Existing shared pools have room for either melee type under the current caps, including their two corpse slots.
